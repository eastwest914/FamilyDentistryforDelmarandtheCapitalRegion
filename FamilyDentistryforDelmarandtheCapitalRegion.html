Common Questions

How often should I visit the dentist?

Many patients benefit from a checkup and cleaning about every six months, though some need visits more often. We recommend a schedule that fits your oral health.


What should I do about a dental emergency?

Call our office as soon as you can during business hours, and we will do our best to see you quickly. For severe swelling or trouble breathing, seek emergency medical care.


Do you offer teeth whitening?

Yes. Professional whitening can brighten stained or dull teeth. We explain the options and what results you can reasonably expect.


What is the difference between a crown and a bridge?

A crown covers a damaged tooth, while a bridge replaces one or more missing teeth by anchoring to neighboring teeth or implants. We help you choose between them.


What payment methods do you accept?
